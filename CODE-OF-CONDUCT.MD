# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We, as contributors and maintainers of **EcoBridge**, pledge to foster an open, welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for everyone participating in this project.

EcoBridge is built to remove barriers between devices and people. That mission extends to our community: collaboration should be respectful, constructive, and accessible to contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels.

---

## Our Standards

### Expected Behavior
Participants are expected to:
- Use respectful, inclusive, and professional language
- Be considerate of differing opinions, cultures, and experiences
- Provide constructive feedback focused on ideas, not individuals
- Accept criticism gracefully and use it to improve the project
- Collaborate in good faith with a shared goal of building high-quality software
- Follow security, privacy, and responsible disclosure guidelines

### Unacceptable Behavior
Unacceptable behavior includes, but is not limited to:
- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind
- Trolling, insulting, or deliberately disruptive comments
- Publishing private, sensitive, or security-related information without permission
- Intimidation, threats, or abusive conduct
- Dismissive behavior toward beginners or contributors with less experience
- Any behavior that compromises community safety, trust, or professionalism

---

## Maintainer Responsibilities
Project maintainers are responsible for:
- Enforcing this Code of Conduct fairly and consistently
- Reviewing issues, pull requests, and discussions respectfully
- Taking appropriate corrective action when violations occur
- Protecting contributors from harassment or hostile behavior
- Upholding EcoBridge’s standards for quality, security, and collaboration

Maintainers reserve the right to remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

---

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies to:
- All project spaces, including GitHub repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and documentation
- All interactions related to EcoBridge, whether online or offline
- Community spaces officially associated with the project

---

## Enforcement
If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it to the project maintainers.

All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with discretion and confidentiality.  
Actions may include warnings, temporary restrictions, or permanent removal from the project, depending on the severity of the violation.

For **security-related issues**, please follow the responsible disclosure process outlined in the README and contact the security email instead of opening a public issue.

---

## Community Values
EcoBridge values:
- Open collaboration over gatekeeping
- Quality over quantity
- Learning and mentorship over ego
- Security, privacy, and user trust above shortcuts

By participating in this project, you agree to uphold these values.
